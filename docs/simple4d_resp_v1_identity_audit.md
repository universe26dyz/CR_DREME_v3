# SIMPLE-4D respiratory identity audit

Reconstruction improvement alone does not establish that the model represents a shared respiratory state. The reported 2k/5k/10k R0 checkpoints show improving reconstruction alongside increasing static DVF, cycle error and latent rank. This analysis does not train or modify the model or checkpoints. There is no external respiratory ground truth.

Each stage is independent: `summary`, `latent`, `surrogate`, `coherence`, `dvf`, `withheld`, `report`, or `all` in dependency order. Each output stage has a manifest, input/checkpoint hashes and raw data backing its figures. Existing stage directories fail closed unless `--overwrite-stage` is specified. The report reads existing stage outputs without inference and checks their input/checkpoint provenance.

Global latent PCA subtracts each location's temporal mean, pools centered posterior means weighted by frame, and eigendecomposes their Gram matrix. The largest absolute loading of each eigenvector is positive. Recomputed PC1/PC2 variance, rank95 and dynamic RMS must agree with checkpoint diagnostics to 1e-4 (rank exactly). All codes are posterior means. `pc1_location_z` divides by the within-location standard deviation; values with standard deviation at most 1e-12 are null.

The image-derived temporal PCA surrogate uses a time-by-time Gram matrix of per-pixel temporally centered frames. Locations with mean temporal pixel variance at most 1e-12 are excluded. It is not a respiratory belt, navigator or ground truth; raw PCA signs are arbitrary and display overlays alone are sign-aligned. Comparisons use absolute location correlations. Representative locations depend on slice position, never on error or correlation.

Cross-view coherence uses location-standardized latent PC1 and mutual-nearest actual timestamps, at 0.25, 0.5 and 1 second. Within-view controls use distinct, ordered locations. A source frame can appear for multiple target locations; coverage counts unique source location/frame identities. Fewer than three pairs or under 10% source coverage is flagged coverage-limited, not assigned a zero correlation.

For DVF, each view's central location with at least five frames provides observed low/median/high-PC1 posterior means. Forward displacement, unclamped Jacobian and inverse cycle are evaluated on a 16³ world grid in chunks. Observation-state volumes evaluate external LPS coordinates through forward deformation and the frozen canonical; no extra frame conversion is applied in the motion model. The NIfTI affine converts external LPS voxel coordinates to RAS metadata. Grid spacing is the validated static resolution; the last voxel may fall below the upper bound when the extent is not divisible by spacing. All displayed volume intensities use [0,1].

The target-code-withheld diagnostic is in-training-set, not held-out generalization. The neighboring-code idea comes from `third_party/simple4d/simple4d/evaluation.py`; timestamp interpolation is a project-specific adaptation: `alpha=(t_target-t_prev)/(t_next-t_prev)` and `z=(1-alpha)*mu_prev+alpha*mu_next`. It uses target geometry, never target code, for V2. Static, V1 and both V2 predictions share PSF samples. Percent changes are `(reference_mse - candidate_mse)/reference_mse * 100`; a positive V2-versus-V1 value means V2 has lower MSE.

The comparison matrix preserves original numerical values and indicates direction per metric; no composite score or authority checkpoint is assigned. Cross-view and image-PCA results are decision support only and cannot independently establish a respiratory phase.

## Periodicity and anatomy-aware DVF

Absolute acquisition timestamps span much longer than a single location's cine series, so absolute-time overlays compress local oscillations. Relative-time traces start at each location's first timestamp. The 0.05–0.6 Hz Lomb–Scargle frequency window is an **audit search range**, not ground-truth physiology. A location needs at least six finite, nonconstant samples, strictly increasing timestamps, and at least 1/0.6 seconds duration. The reported spectral peak is the maximum within this window; a detectable peak means that maximum is an interior local peak, not a statistical significance test. Autocorrelation uses linear interpolation over the observed time interval only, capped at 4096 uniform samples, and selects a positive local peak in the search-period range no later than half the observed duration. These measures provide evidence for or against a respiratory interpretation; they do not establish respiratory phase.

The earlier 16³ full-domain DVF plots were coarse diagnostics, not anatomical ROI images. The new visualization crop is derived from the admitted static reference NIfTI: voxels above 5% of the 99th percentile of positive intensities form a static support mask. Its LPS bounding box is padded by 10 mm, clipped to the effective external domain, and aligned to the validated static spacing; missing, empty or non-intersecting support fails closed. This is an intensity-support crop, **not a heart-specific segmentation**. The manifest flags when padding or support makes the crop equal the full model domain. Source NIfTI path/hash, threshold, shape, bounds, spacing and padding are recorded.

At the same physical crop grid, the frozen canonical supplies the dynamic anatomy background after forward deformation. Magnitude, signed LPS x/y/z components, and high-minus-low vector-difference magnitude are overlaid as heatmaps; cross-checkpoint SAX plots use common scales. The heatmap covers the full crop; a cyan contour marks the static support boundary when one is visible. Statistics distinguish the whole visualization crop from voxels supported by the static mask. No extra LPS/RAS flip is applied inside motion; the NIfTI affine alone maps external LPS coordinates to RAS metadata. GPU evaluation is chunked by `--chunk-size`; temporary full-grid arrays are disk-backed, though percentile/statistics calculations still require host memory. No authority checkpoint is automatically assigned.
